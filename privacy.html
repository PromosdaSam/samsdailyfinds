Privacy Policy

📋 About this document

This policy describes how the Sam's Daily Finds website (samsdailyfinds.com) handles information about its visitors, and how accounts linked to it (Instagram, YouTube) handle data in the context of their integrations with third-party APIs.

📦 Data collected

This site is a static showcase of deals on products — there is no sign-up, login, or form that requests personal data. We do not collect, store, or process any personal information from visitors.

🔗 Affiliate links

Products shown include affiliate links — if you make a purchase through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Curation and publishing of products is automated.

🔐 Use of information (API integrations)

When accounts linked to this project connect to social media APIs, authentication credentials are used solely to publish curated deal content on behalf of the account itself. No data is shared with third parties or used for advertising purposes.

🌐 Hosting and third parties

This site is hosted via GitHub Pages and may use a third-party CDN/DNS service (e.g., Cloudflare) for content delivery. These providers may collect basic technical access data (e.g., IP address, browser type) as a normal part of operating any website on the internet — we do not have access to this data and do not use it for any purpose.

🍪 Cookies

This site does not use its own tracking or analytics cookies.

👶 Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

✏️ Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The last updated date is always shown at the top of this page.
